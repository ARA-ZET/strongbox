BORN IN THE MOTHER CITY

Our Story

StrongBox began with a simple observation: urban living in Cape Town is spectacular, but space is a finite luxury. We realized that traditional self-storage was an industrial, inconvenient chore that didn't fit the modern Capetonian lifestyle.

We envisioned a service that acts as an "Extended Closet"—a natural, premium extension of your home where your belongings are cared for with the same meticulous attention you'd give them yourself. Since our founding, we've focused on combining high-end concierge service with military-grade security.
